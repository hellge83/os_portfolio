## Story 1. Career path: LiveOps → Data Science → ML/R&D → Data Science Lead

> I joined G5 as a LiveOps Manager and was promoted to Head of Department quite quickly. But after some time, I realized that I was more interested in the analytical and technical side of my work than in continuing on a management path.
>
> At the same time, Machine Learning and Data Science were becoming more popular, and I felt that this was something I really wanted to explore. So I decided to develop my analytical skills and started learning Data Science.
>
> I soon began using what I was learning in my daily work and built my first churn model. This also helped start the development of Machine Learning at G5. We began to see that we needed more than just models — we also needed infrastructure to run predictions regularly and tools to use those predictions in the product.
>
> Later, I moved to the Analytics Department, where we built an R&D team with a Data Scientist, an ML Engineer, and a Creative Game Designer. Our goal was to develop and test new game features using data and Machine Learning.
>
> After a while, the company changed its priorities. Instead of focusing on R&D, our team started working more directly on specific product problems together with the Product Owners.
>
> Over time, we realized that many of these problems were connected to LiveOps. I was then offered the opportunity to build and lead a LiveOps Analytics Team. My broad background was very useful in this role because I had experience in ML and Data Science, but also in analytics, statistics, and A/B testing.
>
> So my career path at G5 has been quite unusual. I started in LiveOps, moved into Data Science, helped develop the Machine Learning direction, worked on R&D, and now my role combines leadership, analytics, and hands-on Data Science.
