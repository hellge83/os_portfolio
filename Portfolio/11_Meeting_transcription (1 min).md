### 🔹 Meeting recordings transcription

**SHORT:** *(30 sec)*
> "Smaller side project — a transcription tool for meeting recordings, built to feed into AI agents, so we could convert things like webinars into documentation. I built the MVP myself. Standard tools struggle with fast, continuous Russian speech live, only the summaries they produce are good enough to actually use."
> 
> **STACK:** Python, ffmpeg and two local Hugging Face models for transcription and speaker diarization

**DEEP DIVE:** *(30 sec)*
> "Right now I'm maintaining a custom repository with our A/B testing framework. One of the challenges was that it didn't have good documentation.
> 
> To solve this, I recorded meetings with the original author, transcribed them and used AI to generate structured documentation. After that, I reviewed and edited everything manually.**"
